# Project Overview: Customer Support Assistant (Sample)

## Purpose
The Customer Support Assistant is a sample project for an AI-assisted tool that helps support agents resolve customer inquiries faster by suggesting relevant responses, surfacing related past tickets, and automating routine follow-up tasks.

## Objectives
- Reduce average ticket response time by surfacing suggested replies drawn from past resolved tickets and documentation.
- Reduce agent workload on repetitive requests (password resets, order status, billing FAQs) through automated first-pass responses.
- Improve consistency of support answers across agents by centralizing knowledge in one assistant.
- Provide supervisors with visibility into common issue categories and response quality trends.

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Suggested-reply generation for incoming support tickets (email and chat channels).
- Automated triage/categorization of incoming tickets by topic and urgency.
- A searchable knowledge base the assistant draws from when generating suggestions.
- Basic reporting dashboard on ticket volume, resolution time, and category breakdown.

**Out of scope (for this phase):**
- Fully autonomous ticket resolution without human review.
- Voice/phone support channels.
- Integration with billing or payment-processing systems.

## Key Stakeholders

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Defines priorities, approves scope changes |
| Support Team Lead | Represents end-user (agent) needs, validates suggested-reply quality |
| Engineering Lead | Owns technical architecture and delivery timeline |
| Support Agents | Primary daily users of the assistant |
| Customers | Indirect stakeholders — outcome is measured via resolution time and satisfaction scores |

## At a Glance
A sample project illustrating how an AI assistant could speed up customer support workflows without removing the human agent from the loop — focused on faster, more consistent responses rather than full automation.
