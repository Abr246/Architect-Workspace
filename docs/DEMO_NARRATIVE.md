# GoalKick — Demo Day Narrative

## The problem

Small soccer-field operators run bookings by phone, text, and memory — so double-bookings
happen, pricing and availability questions eat staff time, and refund or complaint
requests sit in someone's inbox with no way to track what was promised or resolved.
Customers get inconsistent answers, and management has zero visibility into which days
are actually busy versus dead. It costs the business bookings, costs staff hours on
repetitive questions, and costs trust when a refund request goes silent.

## The one moment

A customer tries to book a field that's already taken. Instead of a flat rejection, the
system catches the conflict instantly and offers real alternative time slots — each one
independently re-checked against the actual schedule before it's ever suggested, so it's
never offering something that's secretly also booked. The lost booking becomes a
rescheduled one, live, in under a second. That's the "oh": it's not just detecting the
problem, it's already solving it.

## The guardrail

Nothing in this system guesses. Every AI-sounding response is grounded in real booking,
pricing, and availability data — it never invents an answer. When a question falls
outside what it can honestly answer (a refund request, a complaint, anything genuinely
unclear), it says so and hands it to a person instead of making something up — the
human, not the model, has the final word on anything with money or a customer
relationship attached. To show it live: ask it something with no clean data behind it,
and watch it decline honestly and route to staff, rather than confidently answering
wrong. Every one of those handoffs, and every decision a human makes afterward, is
timestamped in an audit trail — so nothing the system does is untraceable, even when
it's a person who made the final call.
