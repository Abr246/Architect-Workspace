    GoalKick

    AI-powered soccer field booking and management platform

    Final Showcase · 8 min speaking · 5 min Q&A (held separately)
    Presenter: Abrahim Nur

    Beat 1 · 0:00–0:50 · The problem and who has it

    One manager. One Saturday. One double-booking.

    • A facility manager runs several soccer fields. Bookings come in by phone, text, and walk-in.
    • Checking a shared calendar by hand is the only thing stopping two people booking the same field, same time.
    • Nobody finds out until someone shows up and there's no field for them.
    • Cost: a refund, an upset customer, and real staff time untangling it — every time it happens.

    Beat 2 · 0:50–3:20 · Live proof

    Watch it happen, live

    1. Book a field the normal way — pick a field, pick a time, confirm.
    2. Try to book that exact same slot again — the system rejects it instantly and suggests real alternative times.
    3. Open Scheduling Issues — the conflict is already listed, with both customers named, ready to resolve in one click.
    4. Ask GoalKick AI: "Are there any scheduling conflicts?" — it answers from the same real data, live.
    5. Open Analytics — the same booking already counted in real bookings, revenue, and busiest-time numbers.

    See demo-runbook.md for the exact starting data and recovery plan.

    Beat 3 · 3:20–4:40 · What AI does and what humans decide

    One rule, drawn on purpose

    The system decides alone

    • Whether a time slot is free
    • Whether a new booking conflicts with an existing one
    • What alternative times to suggest
    • How to answer a factual question from real data

    A person always decides

    • Any refund or complaint (Approvals)
    • What happens to a flagged conflict — cancel, move, or resolve it
    • Anything involving money or an existing customer's booking

    The system flags. A person acts. Every handoff and every human decision is timestamped in the audit trail.

    Beat 5 · 6:00–7:00 · Limits

    Three honest limits

    GoalKick AI is not a language model. It's deterministic logic plus real data — no external API, no key, no guessing. Outside its known questions, it says so.
    The math is real; the dataset is small. Revenue, utilization, and busiest-time figures are computed correctly, but on a small amount of test data — not proven at real-world scale yet.
    Conflict detection covers one case: one field, double-booked. Not staff schedules, not multiple locations. Extending that is a data-model redesign, not a quick add.
